Good Questions Deserve Clear Answers.

Before choosing a home, you should understand what you pay, what you receive, what you own, and what happens if your plans change.

Living in a Glonari Home

Is this ordinary renting?

It is a long-term lease with an option to buy. Glonari owns and manages the residence. You make it your Home, and we take care of owning it. You can stay, explore a transfer to another qualifying Home, or explore buying. It comes with professional management and Gia’s services.

Who owns the home?

Glonari Properties owns and manages the homes in the program. GPA ownership does not give a resident title to an individual residence.

Can I stay for ten years or longer?

The final terms will be set out in the program agreements.

Can I eventually buy my home?

Yes, you can explore buying at then-current value under the purchase terms, with cash or an independently approved mortgage. Purchase valuation and terms must be documented; mortgage approval is separate. GPA does not automatically convert into the Home or its price, and your Home Fee is not credited toward the purchase.

What if Glonari sells the house where I live?

The final terms will be set out in the program agreements.

What if I already own the home I want?

You can sell it to Glonari Properties, or keep it and hold GPA shares instead.

02

Monthly payments

How much does a Glonari Home cost?

In our example, the Home Fee is $7,500 per month for a $1 million residence.

How does that compare with owning with a mortgage?

For the same $1 million reference Home, a traditional ownership budget is $8,155.75 a month: mortgage principal and interest $5,322.42, plus assumed property tax $1,250, insurance $500, upkeep reserve $833.33 and HOA $250. That is about $656 less per month in this example when the Home Fee covers the same costs. These are proposed estimates, not verified quotes.

What does the Home Fee include?

The selected Home agreement sets covered work, exclusions, separate charges and price changes. Do not assume every repair, utility, insurance policy or HOA charge is included.

Can the Home Fee change?

The selected Home agreement sets covered work, exclusions, separate charges and price changes.

Do I get any of my housing payments back if I leave?

No. The Home Fee is a fee for living in the home and its services.

03

GPA and investment

What are GPA shares?

GPA shares are Class A shares in Glonari Properties—an alternative to holding equity in one Home. They combine property and operating-business potential in smaller, transferable units. GPA is company equity, not title to your Home.

Must I buy GPA to live in a Glonari Home?

A Glonari Home is available to Glonari Members who hold GPA equal to at least 20% of the home’s value, or have a sponsor who provides the shares.

Do I have to keep a certain number of shares the whole time?

You hold GPA equal to at least 20% of the home’s value. As GPA appreciates, the minimum number of shares goes down each month. Shares you no longer need can be sold or applied to another property.

Who can invest in GPA?

Participation in GPA and the acquisition of GPA shares depend on applicable requirements and on the investor meeting the established conditions.

What does NAV+ mean?

Our model uses NAV+ to estimate economic value under different scenarios. Three different measures: what has been recognized in financial reporting; how much the economics could be worth (NAV+, management’s supplemental estimate, not a GAAP standard or an audit opinion); and what an eligible buyer will actually pay. The three can differ.

Are GPA shares backed by an individual house?

GPA shares are equity in Glonari Properties. They are not title to a particular residence or a stated lien on an individual house.

Do GPA shares pay regular dividends?

A rising valuation does not create cash. Distributions depend on cash available, legal rights, reserves and approvals.

Can I sell my GPA shares?

You can seek to sell part of your holding. Actual sales depend on transfer rules, buyers and price. GPA has no promised trading market, repurchase program, redemption mechanism, or minimum share price. When shares are sold to an eligible buyer, that buyer pays the selling holder. The company does not automatically receive that cash.

Can I borrow against GPA shares?

An approved credit line (LOC) is a separate loan and pledge, subject to approval and permitted uses. Credit requires a simultaneous share pledge and is not available to everyone. Credit is not assured. A sale of eligible shares or a lender-approved loan secured by shares may provide funds under certain conditions. Neither changes the contractual Home Fee.

What if the value of GPA drops?

Values can fall. Business value depends on executed operations and contracts, and properties and business performance can weaken. After you move in, you are never required to buy more shares. If you use a credit line, falling accepted values may require cash paydown.

Gia & Everyday Living

What is Gia?

Gia, short for Glonari Intelligent Agent, is your personal AI concierge. Gia helps you understand your Home services, bring next steps together and explore the choices ahead.

Does Gia make decisions for me?

You approve commitments. Gia is an AI agent within Glonari technologies and services. Gia does not replace qualified legal, tax or financial advice.

Can Gia arrange a repair?

Gia helps you start the request. The property team checks scope and coverage, and an approved professional does the work. Coverage comes first; you pay only any agreed separate charge. Urgent water or safety issues use the direct emergency process. Coverage and service availability vary.

Can I update my kitchen or bathroom?

Gia helps organize the request. An elective improvement needs an approved scope, quotes, owner approval and a written agreement about who pays. Elective improvements are not assumed to be included in the Home Fee.

Can Gia help me find a home?

Yes. Gia is your personal property advisor. She looks through listings, presents your options and can show what a home could become, with an estimated cost and time. Every property is subject to eligibility, location, condition, title, valuation, and program approval.

What can Gia do today?

The final terms will be set out in the program agreements.

Leaving or moving

Can I move to another Glonari Home?

You can explore another qualifying Home after 12 months, with 90 days’ notice, subject to availability and agreement terms. Glonari transfer fees may apply. Termination follows the agreement.

How is a transfer different from selling?

A Glonari transfer separates your move from selling the residence yourself. Sale and transition costs vary. A 6-10% planning allowance on a $1 million sale is $60,000-$100,000, not a standard commission or promised saving.

What happens to my GPA if I move out?

Your Home agreement and your GPA are separate. If occupancy ends, retain eligible shares or seek a permitted sale. GPA sales require a permitted transaction and buyer; timing and payout are not assured.

What if I decide to leave after ten years?

The final terms will be set out in the program agreements.

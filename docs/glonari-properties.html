Properties

The Company Behind the Homes.

Glonari Properties™ is the real estate business designed to acquire, own, and manage a growing portfolio of residential properties.

Its GPA Class A shares offer eligible investors participation in the broader company—not direct ownership of a particular home.

Explore Glonari Properties

02 / 08

Real Homes. A Broader Business.

Glonari Properties aims to combine residential ownership and management with Gia-powered operations, coordinated purchasing, preventive maintenance, and resident services.

The goal is to improve operating efficiency and create economic value beyond the property alone.

03 / 08

A Better Home. A More Productive Asset.

For the member, a place to live with support. For Glonari Properties, an income-producing residential asset.

Earn more.

The model aims to collect more than rent alone by offering a richer Home experience and adding documented commercial income.

Spend less.

Technology and coordinated purchasing aim to reduce administration and service-delivery costs across the property network.

Create more value.

More sustainable net cash flow can support greater economic value than the physical property alone.

Higher collections and lower costs are operating objectives, not independently demonstrated results. No like-for-like rental benchmark or verified technology savings has been provided.

04 / 08

Own Part of the Business. Not a Particular House.

GPA Class A represents equity in Glonari Properties.

GPA is an alternative to holding equity in one Home. You hold company shares alongside your Home agreement, with growth potential from properties and the operating business. Glonari Members hold GPA equal to at least 20% of the value of their home. GPA shares do not give an investor ownership of the home in which they live.

20%

More capital is not itself a gain. Percentage ownership can fall; later value per share depends on the property and operating economics achieved after new capital is deployed. Participation in GPA and the acquisition of GPA shares depend on applicable requirements and on the investor meeting the established conditions.

07 / 08

Know How You Could Access Your Investment.

You can seek to sell part of your holding instead of an entire property. Smaller purchase sizes can open participation to more eligible buyers than a whole-home sale. That can support liquidity when demand exists; actual sales still depend on transfer rules, buyers and price.

GPA has no promised trading market, listing, redemption, share repurchase, or minimum price.

Borrowing creates repayment obligations and interest. Selling shares reduces the investor’s position and may have tax consequences.

08 / 08

Investment Decisions Deserve Their Own Conversation.

GPA shares can be pledged as collateral for a credit line, if a lender approves. A credit line (LOC) is a separate loan and pledge, subject to approval and permitted uses.

Leverage can increase both gains and losses. Availability of credit, collateral acceptance, interest rates, and investment performance are not guaranteed.
